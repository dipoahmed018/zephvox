The zephvox client

Say it. See what to fix.

zephvox listens while you speak English and shows the sounds and sentences to work on, then lets you practice them until they pass. The small app on your computer records; a zephvox server does the analysis.

Install the client How it works

Free and open source · Linux, macOS and Windows · about 80 MB

How it works

Talk. Get a report. Practice.

  1. Record from anywhere

    Press Ctrl Shift Alt R in any app, talk for a minute or two, and press it again. You don't need the page open.

  2. Your server analyzes it

    The client sends the recording to the zephvox server you connected it to. It transcribes, compares your sounds with the dictionary and checks the grammar, then sends the report back.

  3. Read it, then practice

    See each mistake in its sentence, hear it said correctly, and practice the word until it scores as a pass. Your history stays on your computer.

thorough/θ ʌ ɹ oʊ/
Expected θʌɹoʊ You said tʌɹoʊ

Put the tip of your tongue lightly between your teeth and let the air flow out, with no stop.

Sounds, not just words

See exactly which sound slipped.

Speech apps often just say "try again". zephvox lines up the sounds a word should have against the ones you made, so you can see that the th came out as a t, and what to do with your tongue about it.

What you get

Everything you need to keep improving.

One shortcut, anywhere

Start and stop from any app. A desktop note tells you when your report is ready.

Expected against heard sounds

A dropped or swapped sound shows as a gap or a colored tile, not a vague score.

Grammar, with the fix

The sentence you said, the corrected one and a short reason. Mark a false alarm and it's ignored from then on.

Practice that scores you

Say a word and get a 0–100 score a moment later. It stops listening when you stop talking.

A page that shows what's happening

Wind that follows your voice while you record and carries your words through each step.

Progress over time

Your weakest words and the sounds you mix up most, across every recording.

Works when the server doesn't

If the server can't be reached, recordings wait on your computer and are analyzed when it's back.

Light, dark, and private

Your history stays on your computer, and the page works in light and dark.

Install the client

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. Get an invite

    Ask whoever runs your zephvox server for its address and an invite code. The code is used once, for this one device.

  2. Run one command

    Put in your server's address and your code.

    curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dipoahmed018/zephvox/main/install.sh | sh -s -- \
  --server-url https://speak.example.com --invite inv_...
    $env:ZEPHVOX_SETUP_ARGS = "--server-url https://speak.example.com --invite inv_..."
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dipoahmed018/zephvox/main/install.ps1 | iex

  3. Press the shortcut

    Start zephvox from your app menu, then press Ctrl Shift Alt R and talk. You can change the keys in Settings.

  • A computer running Linux, macOS or Windows. About 80 MB of disk.
  • A microphone, and a connection to your server.
  • Nothing else. The installer brings its own Python through uv. No GPU, no models, no PyTorch.

No server yet? The full app runs everything on your own computer, and can share it with your other devices. See the repository.

Privacy

Your voice goes where you send it, and nowhere else.

Only to your server

Recordings are sent to the one server you connected, and nowhere else. The page warns you if its address isn't encrypted.

Your history stays here

Recordings, reports and practice scores are kept on your computer. Backing them up to the server is a switch in Settings.

A key for each device

Your device's key is kept in a file only you can read. The server's admin can disconnect it, and you can replace it in Settings.

Questions

Before you install.

Do I need a GPU or a fast computer?

No. The client only records and shows reports; the server does the heavy work. Any computer with a microphone is fine.

Where do I get an invite?

From the person or organization that runs the zephvox server you're meant to use. They create your account and send you an invite code and the server's address.

What if the server isn't reachable?

You can keep recording. Recordings wait on your computer, marked "waiting for the server" in History, and are analyzed as soon as it's back.

Can I run everything on my own computer instead?

Yes. In Settings → Where analysis runs, choose This computer. zephvox offers to install the speech models, which are several GB, and then works offline.

How do I change the shortcut keys?

Settings → General → Shortcuts: click Change and press the new keys. You can also bind zephvox toggle in your system's own keyboard settings.

Which English does it check against?

Standard American English by default, or British English if the server is set up for it. Some findings are accent rather than mistakes, and you can mark those as "not a mistake".

How do I remove it?

Run zephvox uninstall. It asks before deleting your recordings.
